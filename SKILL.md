# Personal Agent Micro Break Ocular Strain Ergonomics Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI agents to defend knowledge worker physical hardware, preventing ciliary muscle accommodation spasms, dry eye corneal epithelial erosion, and forward head posture cervical spine overload.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **Dioptric Ciliary Strain Modeling**: Computes constant focal accommodation workload and suppressed blink rate dry-eye acceleration.
- **Hansraj Cervical Torque Mechanics**: Translates forward tilt angle ($0^\circ-60^\circ$) into exact gravitational compressive forces on C5-C7 vertebrae.
- **Micro-Break Scheduling**: Proactively interrupts prolonged continuous screen immersion with the clinically validated 20-20-20 optical protocol.

## Tools Summary

1. `calculate_ciliary_ocular_strain`: Computes diopters, blink rate suppression, and optical micro-break urgency.
2. `calculate_cervical_spine_torque`: Computes cervical spinal loading in kg/lbs and prescribes ergonomic monitor elevation.
